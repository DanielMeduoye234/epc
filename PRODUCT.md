# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pastors, bishops, shepherds, and New Believer officers at Everything by Prayer (EPC) branches. They use the dashboard on Sundays after service and during the week on phones, capturing altar-call names, marking attendance, and following up people who have gone quiet.

Primary jobs on a given screen: record a person, mark who was present, notice who is at risk, and act (call, visit, WhatsApp). Leadership also needs a truthful picture of growth by branch, bacenta, and shepherd.

## Product Purpose

The Everything by Prayer dashboard is EPC's church-growth workspace. It captures New Believers, First Timers, and Members, tracks Sunday attendance, and helps shepherds keep people from slipping away. Success is not a prettier admin panel. Success is every altar-call name stored the same day, weekly attendance logged, and flagged members contacted within 48 hours.

## Brand Personality

Warm, pastoral, practical. The product should feel like a trusted ministry desk: clear, calm, and ready for Sunday rush. Voice is direct and encouraging, never corporate and never cute. Three words: pastoral, clear, dependable.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS church CRMs with navy-and-gold "ministry OS" chrome and empty metric tiles
- Overloaded admin consoles with 20 equally loud nav items and no Sunday-first path
- Playful consumer chat apps (confetti, mascots, slang) sitting on pastoral care data
- Dark "hacker" dashboards and neon analytics skins

## Design Principles

- Sunday speed: the next action (record, mark present, follow up) is obvious in two taps
- Role honesty: a shepherd sees their flock; a recorder sees conversion capture; a bishop sees branches. Do not pretend every user has the same job
- Truth over theatre: never display placeholder stats, fake attendance, or demo-only widgets in live branches
- Pastoral tone, operational density: warmth in copy, density in tables and attendance grids
- Teach in the empty state: if a list is empty, say what to do next, not "nothing here"

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Shepherds often use phones outdoors after service: 44px touch targets, readable contrast on orange-on-white, labels on icon buttons, and no information carried by color or emoji alone. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard access for every modal and the assistant chat.
